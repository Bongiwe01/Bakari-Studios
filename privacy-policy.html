Bakari Studios ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information We Collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you choose to provide: your name, email address, company or organisation, and the content of your message.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information submitted through the contact form solely to respond to your enquiry.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process the information you submit via the contact form on the basis of your consent, given when you complete and submit that form.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any visitor tracking or analytics tools.

Data Retention

Enquiry information submitted via the contact form is kept only for as long as needed to respond to and resolve your enquiry, unless you ask us to retain it for an ongoing business relationship.

Third Parties

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may process visitors' IP addresses as part of delivering those fonts. The contact form is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which receives the information you submit (name, email, company, and message) solely in order to deliver it to us by email. We do not share your information with anyone beyond this.

Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information, where reasonably possible.
  • Object to our processing of your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information you share with us against loss, misuse, or unauthorised access.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at info@bakari.co.za.
